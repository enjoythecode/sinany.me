Thanks for checking out this prototype of my game "Entropy"! All feedback is wholeheartedly appreciated.

Goal of the game: Achieve the milestones in as few ticks as possible.

Game explanation:
- Acquire "entropy" and use entropy to buy buildings that give you more of it.
- Put formulas in all autobuy cells to have the game automatically buy the building with the highest "autobuy value" when you can afford it.
- You can reference other cells and do computation. For example; "E2 / D2", "5 * dashboard!b1", etc.
- do not put an `=` in the front of the formula; everything is parsed as a "formula", even simple values such as "5".

Known issues and bugs:
- Interaction with sheets are unpolished:
- trying to edit cells while game is running breaks sheets
- sorting breaks sheets
- columns can't be fit to content width by double clicking

Roadmap:
- Savefiles so you don't have to re-write formulas everytime
